Common questions

Do you send someone round to look at my boiler before quoting?+

No — that's the whole point of how we work. A clear photo of your boiler's data plate and its surroundings gives our engineers everything they need to quote accurately, without a wasted visit or a salesman in your living room.

What if my photos aren't quite enough to quote from?+

We'll simply message you back asking for one or two more angles, or a specific close-up. It's still all done from your phone — there's no need for anyone to come out.

How quickly can you install a new boiler?+

For genuine no-heat emergencies, we can often fit a replacement the next day. For standard installations, most jobs are booked in within a week of your quote being accepted.

Do you cover my part of Glasgow?+

We install and repair across Greater Glasgow, including the West End, Southside, East End and surrounding towns such as Bearsden, Paisley and Rutherglen. If you're unsure, just ask when you send your photos.
